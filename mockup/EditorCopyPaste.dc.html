How copy and paste works Copy on the selected step takes it and every step under it. One copy at a time: copying another replaces it, and × forgets it. Paste here sits beside every +, the places a step can go, and puts the copy there. The copy stays, so it can go in more than once. Only into this flow: another has its own areas, points and inputs. A pasted step takes a free name, Download buttons 2, since the script names steps. What it refers to inside the copy follows it; one outside it is kept, and flagged when the new place cannot reach it. Buttons only: no Ctrl C and Ctrl V in the tree.
